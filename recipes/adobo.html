Chicken or Pork Adobo

Traditional Filipino chicken or prok adobo is a savory, tangy and garlicky braised dish made by simmering meat in soy sauce, vingegar, garlic, bay leaves and peppercorns.

Ingredients

  • 2 Tablespoon Cooking Oil
  • 6 Cloves Garlic (Crushed)
  • 1 Piece Onion (Sliced)
  • Chicken or Pork Liempo (Cut into Pieces)
  • 3 Tablespoon Vinegar
  • 1/4 Cup Soy Sauce
  • 2 Pieces Bay Leaves
  • 1 Tablespoon Black Peppercorns (Slightly Crushed)
  • 1 Cup Water
  • 1 Piece Knorr Chicken Cube
  • 1 Tablespoon Brown Sugar

Steps

  1. Sauté the onions and garlic in oil for 2 minutes.
  2. Add the chicken or pork pieces to the pot and sear on all sides until the skin is lightly browned.
  3. Pour the vinegar, soy sauce, bay leaves, pepper, water and Knorr Chicken Cube. Simmer for about 20 minutes, do not cover the pot.
  4. Add sugar and simmer for another 6 minutes. Add more sugar or vinegar depending on the taste.
