Arroz Caldo Recipe

Chicken Arroz Caldo

Chicken Arroz Caldo is a type of Filipino rice porridge with chicken. It is seasoned with onion, garlic and fish sauce, and often topped with boiled eggs.

Ingredients

  • 2 Tablespoon Cooking Oil
  • 4 Cloves Garlic (Crushed)
  • 1 Piece Onion (Chopped)
  • 2 Tablespoon Ginger (Chopped)
  • Chicken or Pork Liempo (Cut into Pieces)
  • 2 Tablespoon Fish Sauce
  • 1 Cup Uncooked Rice
  • 4 Cup Water
  • 2 Piece Knorr Chicken Cube

Steps

  1. Sauté the onions, garlic and ginger in oil for 2 minutes.
  2. Add the chicken pieces to the pot and cook until lightly browned. Add fish sauce and cook for around 1 to 2 minutes.
  3. Add the rice while stirring and cook for about 2 minutes.
  4. Add water and bring to a boil. When boiling, add the Knorr Chicken Cubes.
  5. Keep stirring to avoid the rice from clumping together. Let it simmer until the rice has softened.
  6. Season with salt and pepper to taste and top with boiled egg and green onions.
